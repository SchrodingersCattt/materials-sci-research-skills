# Catalytic upgrading of glycerol over supported Ni catalysts

## Results

The treated group reached a glycerol conversion of 87.5 % after 14 days at 220 degrees C, against
61.0 % in the control group (p = 0.003, n = 24 per arm). The catalyst loading was 5.0 wt% Ni on
gamma-alumina. Selectivity toward 1,2-propanediol was 42.5 %.

## Discussion

The authors report that the observed effect may not generalise to field conditions, because the
reactor was operated with a purified feed. No data are reported for catalyst deactivation beyond
the 14-day window.
