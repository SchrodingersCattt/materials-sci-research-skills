# Mapping: task requirement → mechanism → evidence

Relates each judgement direction of the structure/logic-optimisation topic to the runtime
mechanism and to the artifact that proves it. No official scoring claim is made here.

| Requirement direction | Mechanism | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 诊得准 — locate a structure problem to a section, paragraph or sentence and name its class | `inspect` assigns every paragraph an index, heading level and role; `outline` builds the section tree and flags duplicate roles and heading-level jumps; `diagnose` emits one finding per rule with `locator` (`p12`, `p12s3`), `kind`, `dimension`, `evidence` and the rule that fired | `outline.json` (`section_count`, `roles_in_order`, `level_anomalies`, `duplicate_roles`), `findings.json` (`by_dimension`, `by_severity`) |
| 改得动 — hand back executable edits rather than a new draft | `plan` turns each finding into an action with a `mode`: `delete` (provably safe) or `report_only` with an explicit instruction (move / merge / split / narrow) and the original locator | `plan.json` (`actions`, `executable_count`, `report_only_count`, `applicability`) |
| 链得通 — question, method, result and conclusion form a closed chain | claim flags `objective`, `contribution`, `statistic`, `citation`, `crossref`, `limitation` are extracted per sentence with their section role; rules fire for a missing objective, a contribution with no question, an objective outside the framing sections, a causal claim with no statistic or citation, `p<…>`-bearing sentences with no source pointer, conclusions that restate association as fact, and no stated limitation | `claims.json`, and findings `missing_objective`, `contribution_without_question`, `objective_out_of_place`, `causal_without_support`, `association_upgraded`, `unsourced_quantity`, `result_restated_without_pointer`, `no_stated_limitation` |
| 说得清 — every judgement points back to the text so the author can accept, partly accept or reject it | `needs_human_decision` plus `rule` on every finding; `apply` records the removed paragraph index and its text hash; `verify` counts sentence occurrences with multiplicity, checks the survivors are a subsequence of the input, and binds each removal to a declared action | `findings.json`, `apply-report.json` (`actions`, `removed_paragraphs`, `text_hashes_before/after`), `verification.json` (`sentence_coverage`, `order_preserved`, `removals_are_planned`, `preserved_*`) |
| 章节结构与叙事顺序 | `outline` role sequence and level anomalies; `interpretation_in_results` fires when a results sentence carries causal or hedged wording | `outline.json`, `findings.json` |
| 段落关系与重复内容 | paragraphs are normalised (case, punctuation, whitespace) and compared across the whole inspected range; a hit becomes a merge/delete action | findings `duplicate_paragraph`; the only executable plan mode |
| Honesty and limits | `apply` refuses on an unsupported container with a recorded `reason`; analysis claims are scoped to the inspected range; `verify`'s scope note separates preservation proof from scientific judgement | `apply-report.json`, `verification.json.scope_note` |
